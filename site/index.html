Options

Find exact aspect perfectings: moments when the angular separation of two bodies equals the target angle precisely (0° orb).

Every group must hold at the perfecting instant (groups are ANDed); within a group, any one condition suffices (OR). A and B are the pair’s first and second body. Example: Neptune ☌ Pluto with Uranus “co-present with B OR in sign-based opposition to B” = base bodies Neptune + Pluto, aspect Conjunction, plus one group holding those two alternatives. Heliacal phase is judged by elongation from the Sun at that instant: within 8.5° combust, within 15° under the beams, west of the Sun = morning, east = evening.

Find retrograde periods: station to station, extended to the shadow ingress and egress, with the Sun event inside the loop.

A period matches when either station — retrograde or direct — falls in the sign. No sign checked = no filter.

Find sign ingresses: moments when a body crosses a sign boundary (0° of a sign), in direct or retrograde motion.

Only ingresses into the checked signs are shown. No sign checked = no filter.

Method

Positions are geocentric, tropical zodiac. Speed is in ecliptic longitude; its sign gives direct/retrograde motion. Houses: whole-sign. Dates before 1582-10-15 use the Julian calendar. BCE dates back to 12999 BCE are supported: they are read as local mean time at the site longitude (time zones did not exist yet) and always use the Swiss Ephemeris data files, never the built-in approximation. Angles can be shown as degrees/arcminutes/arcseconds or decimal degrees. The Aspects tab finds exact (0° orb) perfectings by bracketing sign changes of the wrapped angular separation on a coarse grid and refining with secant/bisection; the grid step adapts to the fastest selected pair (5 days with the Moon in play, up to 120 days for outer planets — a step keeping the separation change under 45° provably brackets every transverse root). The Retrograde tab scans speed-zero crossings the same way on a 5-day grid and groups them into shadow-extended periods. The Ingresses tab finds sign-boundary crossings on a grid sized to the fastest selected body (under 15° of motion per step, so no boundary is skipped). Aspect searches accept optional conditions (judged at the perfecting instant; groups are ANDed, conditions within a group are ORed): signs of the pair or any other body by sign, traditional ruler, element, quadruplicity, or co-presence; extra aspects by orb or by whole sign; direction of motion; speed percentile against 1800–2300 statistics; and heliacal phase by solar elongation (combust within 8.5°, under the beams within 15°, west of the Sun = morning, east = evening). Search ranges are unbounded — outer-planet aspects over centuries are fine, though very long ranges take a while to scan. Every results table has a CSV download button at its top. The positions table also shows each planet’s previous and next station (Mercury–Pluto, scanned over a window set by Mars — the longest station cycle at ±850 days), the bracketing new/full moons, and — for the seven classical planets — the decan lord (Chaldean order, or the Indian triplicity-domicile sequence), Egyptian bound lord, Dorothean triplicity lords (by day/night sect), exaltation lord, and the Dodecatemoria (D12) and Novenaria (D9) sub-lords. The lordship deep dive below the lots gathers, for each classical planet, the lords over it and everything it rules — domicile, exaltation, decan, bound, triplicity, D12/D9 lordship over the other classical planets, the angles and the lots, plus whole-sign houses ruled.
